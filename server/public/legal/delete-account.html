Delete your PulseQuiz account

Last updated September 21, 2026

In the app: Settings → Danger Zone → Delete My Account, then type DELETE to confirm. Deletion is immediate.

Without the app: email support@pulsequiz.app from the email address of your Apple, Google or Facebook sign-in, with the subject “Delete my PulseQuiz account” and your username. We verify the request and delete the account within 30 days.

What is deleted

Your profile (email, name, username, avatar, provider ID, prize wallet), quiz history, streaks, friends, challenges, push tokens and activity log. Your coin balance and any unpaid prizes are forfeited.

What is kept

Anonymised purchase and payout records, retained as long as tax and accounting law requires (typically up to 7 years), with no link to your identity.
