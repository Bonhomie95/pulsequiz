Support

Last updated September 21, 2026

Need help? Email support@pulsequiz.app with your username and a description of the problem. We usually reply within 2 business days.

Common questions

I paid but didn’t get coins. Open Wallet → Buy Coins and tap “Paid but didn’t receive coins? Tap to complete”. Pending purchases are also completed automatically the next time you open the app.

How do I restore Premium? Open Premium and tap “Restore Purchases”.

How do I cancel Premium? In your App Store or Google Play subscription settings, or via “Manage or cancel subscription” in the app.

Why wasn’t I paid a prize? Your Wallet screen shows every requirement. See the Official Rules.

How do I delete my account? Settings → Delete My Account, or request deletion on the web.
