Privacy Policy

Last updated September 22, 2026

This policy explains what PulseQuiz collects, why, who we share it with, and your choices.

What we collect

DataWhy
Email address and name from Apple, Google or Facebook sign-in; your provider account IDCreate and secure your account
Username and avatar you chooseShow you on leaderboards, PvP and friends lists
Gameplay: answers, scores, streaks, matches, challenges, tournamentsRun the game, leaderboards, anti-cheat
Purchase records (product, store transaction ID, time)Deliver and verify purchases, handle refunds
Prize wallet: chosen coin (USDT/USDC), network and wallet address; payout historyPay prizes you win
Push notification tokenSend streak, prize and challenge notifications (you can turn these off in system settings)
Device and advertising identifiers, coarse location derived from IP (collected by Google AdMob)Show ads; personalised only if you consent (iOS App Tracking Transparency / consent form)
Crash reports and performance dataFind and fix bugs
IP address and timezoneSecurity, fraud prevention, and your local day boundary for streaks and the Daily Quiz
Country (from network location or device region)Showing cash prizes only where they are offered, and prize eligibility

We do not sell your personal data.

Who we share it with

  • Apple, Google, Meta — sign-in, and Apple/Google for purchase verification.
  • Google AdMob — advertising (not shown to Premium subscribers except rewarded ads you choose to watch).
  • NOWPayments — our payout processor receives your wallet address, coin, network and prize amount to send prizes.
  • Sentry — crash reporting. Expo — push notification delivery. MongoDB Atlas — database hosting.
  • Other players see your username, avatar, level and scores.
  • Authorities, when required by law.

Retention

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account we delete your profile, quiz history, friends, challenges, devices and activity, and anonymise the account. Purchase and payout records are kept in anonymised form for as long as tax and accounting law requires (typically up to 7 years). Crash reports are kept for up to 90 days.

Your rights

You can access, export, correct or delete your data. Delete your account in Settings → Delete My Account or at /delete-account. For an export or any other request, email support@pulsequiz.app. You can withdraw ad-tracking consent in your device settings.

Children

PulseQuiz is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data. Prizes are only for players aged 18+.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Sessions can be revoked, and changes to your prize wallet trigger an alert and a 72-hour payout hold.
