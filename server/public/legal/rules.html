Official Rules — PulseQuiz Leaderboard Prizes

Last updated September 22, 2026

NO PURCHASE NECESSARY. A purchase does not improve your chances of winning. Void where prohibited or restricted by law. This competition is sponsored solely by PulseQuiz. Apple Inc. and Google LLC are not sponsors of, and are not involved in, this competition in any way.

1. Eligibility

Open to registered PulseQuiz players aged 18 or older (or the age of majority where they live, if higher) in jurisdictions where skill-based contests with cryptocurrency prizes are lawful. Prizes are offered only in the countries where the app shows them; in other countries the leaderboards still run but carry no prize. We determine your country from your network location and device region, and a winner may be asked to confirm it. Players are responsible for complying with local law. One account per person. Accounts must be at least 7 days old and have completed at least 5 quizzes, and must not be under an open fair-play review.

2. How to take part

Play Ranked quizzes or 1v1 matches in the app. Each correct answer earns leaderboard points; answering all 10 questions of a run correctly earns a 10-point bonus. Answers given with the help of a hint or extra time (bought with coins) earn no leaderboard points and void the bonus, so purchases can never improve ranking. Only the first 20 ranked quizzes per day count toward the leaderboard. Practice runs, the Daily Quiz, friend challenges and weekly leagues earn no leaderboard points and never carry cash prizes (league rewards are coins only).

3. Periods

Weekly periods run Monday 00:00 UTC to the following Monday 00:00 UTC. Monthly periods run from 00:00 UTC on the 1st of each month to 00:00 UTC on the 1st of the next.

4. Prizes and winners

The number of paid ranks is shown in the app during the period; prize amounts are revealed when the period closes. Winners are the highest-ranked eligible players by leaderboard points at the close of the period. Ties are resolved by the leaderboard's ordering at close. Prizes are paid in USDT or USDC (the winner's choice in Settings) on the supported network the winner selects. Prizes under the minimum payout amount (currently US$5) accumulate until the total reaches it.

5. Payment

Payouts are sent automatically after a period closes to the wallet saved in the app. A winner with no wallet saved, or whose wallet was changed within the previous 72 hours, is not paid for that period. PulseQuiz is not responsible for funds sent to an address or network entered incorrectly. Winners are responsible for any taxes.

6. Fair play

Cheating, automation, multiple accounts, or exploiting bugs results in disqualification and forfeiture of prizes. Accounts flagged for unusual activity are held for manual review before payment.

7. Coins

Coins are virtual items with no cash value. They are not prizes, cannot be exchanged for USDT/USDC, and cannot earn leaderboard points.

8. Changes

PulseQuiz may change prize pools for future periods. A period already in progress will not be changed to players' detriment.
